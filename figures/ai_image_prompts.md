# AI Image Generation Prompts for MEISAI Project

Use these prompts in tools like Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Stable Diffusion to generate high-quality diagrams matching the "6-Phase Model Framework" structure.

## Global Style Settings (Append to all prompts)
> **Style:** High-end academic schematic, isometric 3D info-graphic, clean vector lines, white background, pastel color palette (professional blue, orange, slate grey). High resolution, 8k, unreal engine 5 style rendering of a data flowchart. No text labels (or minimal lorem ipsum), focus on structure and flow.

---

## Problem 1: Fan Vote Reconstruction (Bayesian/Inverse Model)
**Concept:** A reverse-engineering pipeline. Data flows in, gets "un-mixed" through a probabilistic filter, and emerges as clean distribution curves.

**Prompt:**
> A complex 6-phase isometric technical diagram of a Bayesian Inverse Problem solving pipeline. Left-to-right flow.
> **Phase 1 (Input):** Raw dirty data blocks entering a funnel.
> **Phase 2 (Formulation):** Mathematical equation symbols and probability distribution curves floating in a glass structure.
> **Phase 3 (Optimization):** A glowing "Simulated Annealing" core, visualizing a search algorithm finding a minimum valley.
> **Phase 4 (Processing):** Data passing through linear filters, becoming organized.
> **Phase 5 (Validation):** Comparison of two curves overlapping perfectly.
> **Phase 6 (Output):** Clean, sorted bar charts and error margin bands.
> **Visuals:** glowing nodes, connection wires, glass texture layers.

## Problem 2: Mechanism Comparison (Game Theory Logic)
**Concept:** Two parallel systems running side-by-side. One is rigid (Ranking), one is fluid (Percentage). They merge into a simulation engine.

**Prompt:**
> A structural diagram comparing two parallel processing systems. Left-to-right flow.
> **Phase 1 (Setup):** A central database node splitting into two distinct pipelines.
> **Phase 2 (Logic):** Two parallel paths. Top path is rigid, stepped blocks (representing Ranking). Bottom path is fluid, smooth curves (representing Percentage).
> **Phase 3 (Simulation):** The two paths feed into a complex central engine gear system (Counterfactual Simulation).
> **Phase 4 (Conflict):** Sparks or red warning nodes indicating "Controversy" or "Conflict" detection.
> **Phase 5 (Metrics):** Dashboard gauges showing "Fairness" and "Reversal Rate".
> **Phase 6 (Policy):** A final document output with a "Stamp of Approval" or trade-off balance scales.
> **Visuals:** Blue vs Orange color coding for the two paths, mechanical aesthetics.

## Problem 3: Dancer Effect Analysis (Dual Regression/Causal)
**Concept:** Statistical inference. Separating signal (Dancer) from noise (Judge). Matrices and scatter plots embedded in the flow.

**Prompt:**
> A sophisticated statistical modeling schematic. Left-to-right flow.
> **Phase 1 (Data):** Matrix grids and tables being stacked.
> **Phase 2 (Spec):** A branching decision tree showing "Model Selection" choices.
> **Phase 3 (Estimation):** Two distinct regression planes (3D scatter plots with fitted planes) side-by-side. One labeled "Judge Bias", one "Dancer Skill".
> **Phase 4 (Diagnostics):** Residual plots and bell curves checking for errors.
> **Phase 5 (Inference):** A magnifying glass focusing on specific coefficient bars.
> **Phase 6 (Output):** A finalized "Effect Size" chart showing impact factors.
> **Visuals:** Mathematical elegance, floating grids, regression lines, clean white aesthetic.

## Problem 4: Fairness Optimization (System Control)
**Concept:** A control loop. Feedback mechanisms, optimizing dials, landing on a Pareto efficiency curve.

**Prompt:**
> An optimization control system diagram. Left-to-right flow with a feedback loop.
> **Phase 1 (Goals):** distinct target icons (Fairness, Stability, Excitement).
> **Phase 2 (Metrics):** Mathematical formula blocks being constructed.
> **Phase 3 (Design):** A mixing board with sliders and dials (Mechanism Design).
> **Phase 4 (Optimization):** A 3D surface plot showing a "Hill Climbing" or "Grid Search" process finding the peak.
> **Phase 5 (Stress Test):** The system being hit by "lightning" or random noise (Robustness Check), staying stable.
> **Phase 6 (Deployment):** A golden "Optimal Solution" cube being delivered.
> **Visuals:** Future-tech interface, green and gold accents for "Optimization", feedback loops arrows.
